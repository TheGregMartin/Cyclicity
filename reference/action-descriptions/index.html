Action descriptions

Drone Factory

As a non-voting action, a player may place 1 drone from behind their screen onto their Drone Factory (even if one or more drones are already present). Drones on the Drone Factory are never automatically removed. However, the player may choose at any time to remove as many drones as they like, returning them behind their screen; for each 3 drones (rounded down) so removed, the player gains an additional drone from their reserve.

Mining Operations

As a non-voting action, a player may place 1 drone from behind their screen onto their Mining Operations (even if one or more drones are already present). Drones on the Mining Operations are never automatically removed. However, the player may choose at any time to remove as many drones as they like, returning them behind their screen; for each 2 drones (rounded down) so removed, the player gains a supply ship from the reserve.

Objective Rule cover tile

There are three separate action spots on this tile; each one has no effect other than to leave the upside-down drone there for the rest of the game. Only one of the actions can be used per branch point vote.

At the end of the game, if there are three drones from the same polity on this tile, that polity may mark one Polity Disposition box corresponding to the name of the Classified Polity Objective under this tile (or gain VP from overfilling), just as if they had drafted that objective themselves.

Patent Office

As a non-voting action, a player may place 2 drones from behind their screen onto the Patent Office. When they do so, they take the Patent Agent tile, placing it in a publicly visible area.

  • It is legal for a player already possessing the Patent Agent tile to take this action, to block anyone else from taking the tile from them this branch point vote.

Surveillance Network

As a non-voting action, a player may place 3 drones from behind their screen onto the Surveillance Network.

  • When they do so, they place a 4th drone from behind their screen onto the surveillance corner of the Sponsor tile. If they do not have a 4th drone available, they cannot take this action.
  • A player taking the Surveillance Network action does not deploy a Voting or Abstain tile that turn (in effect, taking this action postpones their turn).

When the voting turn would pass to the player holding the Sponsor tile, and there is a drone on its surveillance corner, the player whose drone it is reclaims it to behind their screen and takes a single turn as if they were seated to the right of the Sponsor.

  • All other turns are taken in their normal order. In particular, if the Sponsor tile moves far enough, it is possible for the surveilling player to take their turn in the normal order before getting to take their surveillance turn.
  • If the surveillance turn moves the Sponsor tile, players continue taking turns as usual starting with the player who had held the Sponsor tile before the surveillance turn. If the surveillance turn does not move the Sponsor tile, the voting phase ends as usual.
