Game concepts

Action spot

An action spot is a location on a rule card, installation, or mat—typically outlined by a red rectangle with rounded corners—that a player can use to take a non-voting action during a branch point. Action spots have icons that denote their cost, frequency of use, and other details of the action’s use.

Behind a player’s screen

Drones, supply ships, and other assets kept behind a player’s polity screen are available for the player’s use, while being kept unseen by other players. This is in contrast, for example, to assets in reserves, which are not active or available and are simply pools from which new assets can enter the game.

Bribes

Whenever a player has a choice of actions to take (typically, but not exclusively, during branch points), other players can bribe them with supply ships to take or not take a particular action. The player taking the action and the player(s) offering the bribe agree upon a specific action and a specific number of supply ships (which the briber(s) must have behind their screen). When they are in agreement, the briber(s) give the supply ships to the player taking the action, who is then obligated to take the agreed-upon action.

  • All bribery negotiations are conducted aloud in front of all players.
  • The action must be one that the player could normally choose to take.
  • Assets other than supply ships cannot be used in bribes. Drones, installations, tiles, and other assets cannot be given from one player to another unless explicitly allowed by a game rule.

Deploying tiles

On a player’s first turn of a branch point, they must deploy either a Voting tile (with its 0 or 1 side face up) or an Abstain tile.

  • If their first action is voting for one of the branch point outcomes with one or more drones, they must deploy the corresponding Voting tile. In later turns of that branch point, they may freely choose to add drones to their Voting tile (but not to change which side of it is face up) or to take non-voting actions.
  • If their first action is a non-voting action, they must deploy their Abstain tile. In later turns of that branch point, they may only take non-voting actions.
  • If a player takes the Surveillance Network action on their first turn, that turn is treated as postponed to a later time, and the player does not deploy a Voting or Abstain tile at that point.

Drone reserve

Each player has a drone reserve which holds all of their polity’s drone tokens that are not in play. While the drone reserve is physically located behind their polity screen, the drones in it are not considered “behind their screen” for being available to play. Players only gain drones from their reserve when explicitly allowed by a game rule.

Installation

An installation is a square tile gained by a player as a reward (most commonly for Sponsoring a branch point decision) that is played on the player’s installation mat. Installations have effects that can trigger at various times during a game. See Zones below for rules governing the positioning of installations on an installation mat.

Momentum

Each of the six sliders has, at all times, a momentum value that is one of –2, –1, 0, +1, or +2. These momentum values are marked by momentum tokens placed on the slider; the absence of a momentum token on a slider means that the slider has momentum 0. When resolving a branch point outcome:

  • If a slider with a positive momentum (+1 or +2) has its value raised by the outcome, its value increases by an additional 1.
  • Each slider whose value was raised by the outcome has its momentum set to +2.
  • Each slider whose value was not raised by the outcome has its momentum reduced by –1.
  • If a slider with momentum –2 ever has its momentum reduced, instead the slider’s value itself is reduced by –1.

Polity reward

Each polity has several rewards available on the inside of its polity screen, denoted by gold rectangles containing a white number. These rewards are earned either by accumulating Progress Points, by completing the polity’s Narrative Achievement, or by marking all boxes on the polity’s uppermost Polity Achievement.

When a polity reward is earned, the player consults their polity reward card with the corresponding number (kept in a polity-specific envelope in the game box) to determine the exact reward.

Supply ship reserve

The supply ship reserve holds all supply ship tokens that are not in play. It can be placed in any publicly accessible location(s) convenient for the players. Players only gain supply ships from the reserve when explicitly allowed by a game rule.

Zones

Each player’s installation mats contains rows labeled Inner Zone, a Middle Zone, and an Outer Zone. These Zones are where installations are kept. (The bottom row begins the campaign not in play.) Within a particular Zone, a player may rearrange the order of its installations whenever they like; on the other hand, a player cannot choose to change the Zone an installation is in.

At all times, a player’s installations must obey the “gravity rules”:

  • Installations within a particular Zone must be as far to the left as possible;
  • Any installation in the Middle or Outer Zone must have an installation directly beneath it.

Every time a player gains an installation, they must place it in a Zone in a way that obey these rules. If a player ever loses an installation or otherwise finds themselves in a situation where they cannot obey these rules, they move one installation in the Middle or Outer Zone with a blank space directly beneath it into that blank space.
