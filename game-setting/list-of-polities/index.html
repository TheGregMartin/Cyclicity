 Achaemenia

Established 2000 years ago, Achaemenia claims the original Malmani as one of their own, although they were slow to fully embrace their ideology of peace and equality. They are a highly efficient, multicultural polity that values linguistic homogeneity, excels at infrastructure, and likes to be the first to adopt new energy technologies. They are known for slow and steady decision-making, and have a tendency to blame any shortcomings on the equipment or substances rather than on their own systems. They value their biological unity but are accepting of individualized cybernetic enhancement.

  Aksum

Aksum started out as a commercial power sixteen centuries ago, profiting from local iron extraction, ivory, incense, gold, and exotic animals, and constructing great monuments from granite columns. Today, their society is highly individualistic, embracing flashy personal adornment and advanced prosthetic limbs and organs, and conducting its affairs with flamboyance and ceremony. They generally trust traditional mechanical solutions and energy sources, and are content to let the younger polities hustle for recognition, in part to ensure that others treat them with as much respect as the oldest polities.

  Ascomanni

Ascomanni began 700 years ago as a community of skilled seafaring traders and raiders, before becoming the last polity (along with Heian) to be visited by a Malmani prophet. They value homogeneity in their population and excel at experimental energy sources; their interest in genetic engineering stems from surviving a dangerous disease that struck their people in the past. They frown upon cybernetic enhancement and disdain artificial intelligence, preferring to make as many of their own decisions as possible, and believe that concentrating on one science at a time yields better results.

  Gupta

Founded after their Malmani prophet visited 1100 years ago, Gupta has since become known for their academic scholars, astronomers, and the intellectual board game caturaṅga. Their arts, particularly their epic poetry, became the height of literature and remain peerless, and they maintain vibrant trade ties with other polities. They have a strong interest in artificial intelligence and prefer to improve their minds over their bodies, while still believing in distributing scientific support equally among fields. Despite an affinity for modern energy solutions, they have a strong desire to be treated as one of the older polities.

  Han

Han emerged 1500 years ago from a confederation of smaller states, consolidating into three allied kingdoms. Their excellence in salt and paper production and hydraulic constructions attracted the visit of their Malmani prophet 1100 years ago. With an emphasis on engineering, particularly in the development of artificial intelligence and futuristic materials, Han tends to let younger polities risk experimental energy sources and deliberate genetic modifications while they focus on proven advancements. They tend to shift their attention from one new technology to another whenever they see the need for prudent leadership.

  Heian

With a reputation for literature and calligraphy, as well as verdant lands and islands, Heian was the last polity (along with Ascomanni) to receive a Malmani prophet 600 years ago. They stimulate advances in artificial intelligence and prefer tangible expression to prolonged abstract theorizing. They prefer honoring their heritage over chaotic change, maintaining tried-and-true energy production and only adopting cybernetic improvements once they are well tested. They aim to make as many decisions as swiftly as possible and to fund all types of sciences equally, believing that each discipline has its own unique contributions to make.

  Inca

Inca is a civilization that has thrived for nearly 300 years in difficult terrains, with well-developed agriculture, road systems, and textile production despite the forbidding landscape. One of the three youngest world powers along with Tu'i Tonga and Seljuk, they leave fundamental theory to older polities while adopting cutting-edge building materials and modernizing as fast as possible. They prefer to support a single type of science at a time so as to maximize innovation and speed of implementation. They take pride in their culture, ensuring that other polities know that present-day Incan influence is vital to the world and the future.

  Inuit

Inuit traces its origins back six centuries to the earliest human settlements in the far northern reaches of the world. Known historically for hunting, survival skills, insulating materials, and animism, they are the oldest polity not to have been visited by a Malmani prophet. They embrace natural evolution coupled with available cybernetics, and they promote the development of new materials for technological progress. They prefer quick decisions and rapid implementation like many of the newer polities, and they generally aim to promote a single discipline above all others for efficiency and practicality.

  Maya

The oldest polity, Maya was already known 3500 years ago for their crop cultivation, monuments, and mastery of mathematics and astronomy. They embraced the peace and equality taught by their Malmani prophet, whom they claim was the original Malmani themselves, and have become world leaders in fields such as orbital mechanics and large-scale construction. The Mayan people are robustly varied and, above all else, patient. They reject the culture of routine individual genetic manipulation common in many other polities, and even prefer to avoid cybernetics when possible.

  Oyo

Once a rapidly expanding hierarchical society, Oyo embraced Malmani's secrets of longevity but initially resisted their other messages of peace and acceptance, and Malmanism never really replaced their ancestral Yoruba religion. They make little investment in long-term research or speculative energy exploration, but rather are known for their genetic expertise, particularly in animal breeding both historical and modern-day. Preferring to work in visible and noteworthy disciplines, they were the primary architects of the Orbital Research Installation, and they wish to be regarded as the pinnacle of scientific progress.

  Scythia

Once an ancient nomadic equestrian empire older than all other polities save Maya, Scythia eventually settled down to establish stable settlements and engage in trade once visited by their Malmani prophet, whom they claim was the true Malmani. Their success in agriculture led to a passion for genetic engineering and directed evolution, and they maintain the widespread use of horses and traditional energy sources. They are uneasy with overdependence on material resources, but their cultural perseverence makes them excellent and purposeful researchers.

  Seljuk

Amidst conflict among neighboring powers, the youngest polity Seljuk was deliberately formed 200 years ago as a buffer region, evenly split between two continents, that marked the cessation of interpolity war. Their affinity for artificial intelligence is surpassed only by their distaste for bionic prosthetics. Known for their universities, astronomy, and support for fundamental research, Seljuk is an eager member of the Interpolity Science Assembly, recommending ample funding and demanding quick results from those granted the ISA's resources.

  Theodosia

Theodosia began 1200 years ago as a polity skilled in engineering. After being visited by their Malmani prophet preaching their gift of longevity, they introduced the first treatment hospitals and spurred public health to flourish; the benefits were so great that they codified Malmanism as the official polity religion for many centuries. With little cultural appetite for theoretical exploration, Theodosia now focuses on practical strengths such as AI-controlled optical telegraphs and world centers for genetic treatments. They believe in sharing their breakthroughs with the rest of humanity for the common good.

  Tu’i Tonga

Tu'i Tonga originated from a collection of ocean-exploring tribes which gradually merged 400 years ago into a cohesive culture despite spanning hundreds of islands. One of the youngest three polities, they now enthusiastically explore and settle throughout the solar system, adopting new technologies to power their spaceships. While discouraging convergent genetic modifications in their own population, they value stimulating a diverse array of research and are eager to fund as many sciences as possible, often looking to quickly complete and be recognized for a signature accomplishment.

  Umayyad

Umayyad rose to prominence as a maritime trading power and a centre of philosophy and multiculturalism. With help from their neighbours, they embraced secrets of longevity and promoted medical achievements and welfare programs, leading to civic expansion and a tradition of beneficial scientific research. Umayyad celebrates a diverse population and collaborates on global projects to increase its acceptance by older polities. They have eagerly followed their neighbors into the age of cybernetic enhancement, and they would rather catalyze outer solar system exploration from everyone than be solely responsible for it.
