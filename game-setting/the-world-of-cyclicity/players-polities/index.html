Players’ polities

Achaemenia (Graham)

One of the oldest three polities, Achaemenia began two millenia ago as one of many aggressive regional tribes. As their influence spread around the Oqyanuse coastline, they created an efficient centralized bureaucracy to administer the lands within their sphere; over time they added an infrastructure of roads, civil services, and even a postal system throughout their empire. As a result, Achaemenia became an interesting combination of a multicultural population speaking one official language. Achaemenia claims the original Malmani, whom they say visited them seventeen centuries ago, as one of their own (although Achaemenians are willing to stipulate that this true Malmani then traveled to Scythia afterwards); it is a point of pride that the polity did not require a second Malmani visit, as the other two oldest polities did. Achaemenia continued to intermittently expand and shrink through various conflicts until a sudden societal near-collapse six centuries ago; some say that only in its rebuilding did it truly embrace Malmani's ideology of peace and equality. Achaemenia continues to excel at infrastructure and is typically the first to adopt and spread new energy technology, unlike the other older polities. Centuries of intrapolity reproduction have produced a homogeneous appearance among Achaemenians, a biological unity that they value, although they tend to be more accepting of individualized cybernetics (even more so for people who travel widely throughout the solar system). Governance is characterized by determination and by slow and steady decision-making, with a recurring tendency to blame any shortcomings on the equipment or substances rather than on their own systems. Among the arts, Achaemenia is famously dismissive of song and poetry.

Gupta (Katie)

Unlike most other polities, Gupta's origins actually begin with the visit from their Malmani prophet eleven centuries ago: emissaries from nearby Han and Achaemenia spoke in favor of a local maharani gifted in political administration, who received their secrets of longevity to spread to her people. With peace and acceptance built into their polity from the beginning, Gupta quickly became noted for its academic scholars, particularly its astronomers; their strategy game caturaṅga, played on a grid board, spread rapidly and still has no intellectual equal. The arts absolutely flourished, from architecture and sculpture to painting and plays; most notably of all, Gupta's puranas (epic cultural poems) became the height of literature, shrugged at within the inelegant Achaemenian culture but appreciated by all other polities. The Gupta people are comprised of innumerable different cultures and languages (even to this day), and they embrace the biological differences that emerge from their variety. Often described as having their heads in the clouds, the Gupta share a keen interest in artificial intelligence with Han; they are less fond of detail-oriented disciplines that require intricate materials or mechanisms, and prefer to improve their minds rather than their bodies' capabilities. Still, Gupta believes that scientific support should be distributed as equally as possible, and they use their strong trade ties with other polities to support their neighbours' projects. As Gupta is barely a millenium old, they have a strong desire to be considered one of the older polities (despite the affinity for modern energy solutions that they share with Achaemenia), and so they often conciously adopt a benevolent attitude of letting younger polities hustle for recognition.

Inuit (Vivien)

Inuit traces its origins back six centuries to the earliest human settlements in the far northern reaches of the world. With a focus on hunting and survival skills, their society developed in harmony with the harsh, frozen Lu'um environment they called home. Animism played a significant role in Inuit's founding beliefs and practices, with a deep respect for the spirits of the land, water, and sky that provided for their livelihoods. Inuit is the oldest polity that did not receive a visit from a Malmani prophet, possibly due to a devastating plague that raced into their eastern regions at the time when they were becoming most politically cohesive. Instead, they were sponsored to join the ranks of existing world powers by their neighbors Ascomanni and Heian, leading to a rapid expansion of their influence. Inuit remains true to their roots and embraces their people's natural evolution without resorting to genetic intervention. They are keen to adopt cybernetics as they become available, and they actively support the development of new materials and technologies for their progress, just as they developed innovative insulating materials to sustain themselves through their earliest years. With a culture that prizes efficiency and practicality, Inuit aims to promote a single discipline above all others, focusing their resources on one that is most likely to produce tangible results. Quick decisions and rapid implementation are valued, but only when they come from human minds, as Inuit is wary of relying too heavily on artificial intelligence. Their animistic beliefs serve as a reminder to never lose sight of the spiritual and emotional needs of their people, ensuring that progress is always tempered with empathy and understanding.

Oyo (Barney)

Originally a single great city nine centuries ago in western Alkebulan, Oyo soon gathered its warriors and superior cavalry and underwent a rapid expansion of land and wealth. When their Malmani prophet visited two hundred years later, accompanied by Aksum emissaries, Oyo was a strongly hierarchical society whose organizational skills and commitment to their superiors facilitated the accomplishment of a great many simultaneous polity projects. While Malmani's secrets of longevity were eagerly accepted, their other messages of peace and acceptance took longer to be embraced, and Malmanism never really replaced their ancestral Yoruba religion. Still, over time Oyo's changes in leadership became less drastic, as the original practice was discontinued of unpopular sovereigns being forced by cultural honour codes to commit suicide. Oyo innovators tend to seek the centre of attention when projects are undertaken, and the polity prefers disciplines where end results are visible and noteworthy. Exemplifying this attribute, Oyo was the primary source of construction for the Orbital Resarch Installation (and their population knows that the acronym ORI was intentionally chosen to be the same as the Yoruba word for a person's spirit). Like their Theodosian neighbours, Oyo makes little investment in long-term research or speculative energy exploration, although they don't bother denying that their history of espionage is still active enough to benefit from others' discoveries. Oyo's genetic expertise, ultimately stemming from their original animal breeding success, is respected throughout the world; its leaders at all levels encourage the populace to experiment with new gene therapies in the hopes that Oyo will ultimately be seen as the pinnacle of human development.

Scythia (Janelle)

Older than all other polities with the exception of Maya, Scythia began its existence two and a half millenia ago as a nomadic equestrian empire with a lifestyle free of any fixed location, spreading their influence by dominating farming settlements and extracting tribute from them. They kept their demands reasonable, though, and over time acquired and shared mastery of agricultural breeding to improve the crops of those who stayed with their empire. For a while Scythia was known for its metallurgy, but the craft was eventually associated with several serious military defeats, giving rise to an uneasiness with dependence on material resources that persists to the present day. Increased contact with their Achaemenian neighbours half a millenium after Scythia's origin stimulated trade and the establishment of more stable settlements. Scythia claims that their Malmami prophet, who first visited them seventeen centuries ago, was the original Malmani themselves (although Scythians are willing to stipulate that this true Malmani then traveled to Achaemenia afterwards), who passed on their secrets of longevity and cultivation. When Malmani visited again, half a century later and essentially unaged, the polity wholly embraced their ideology of peace and equality as well. Scythia's traditional success with crops has evolved into a cultural passion for genetic engineering of plants, animals, and humans alike, as well as an eager embrace of directed evolution; their cultural perseverence makes them excellent and purposeful researchers in general. Scythia is unique among the modern polities in that horse usage is still widespread, and they generally see no need to adopt newfangled energy sources when the tried and true ones suffice.

Tu’i Tonga (Jason)

Tu'i Tonga's origins are as a collection of ocean-exploring tribes around Fonua and the islands of the southern Taipingyang, which began coalescing four centuries ago into a single widespread culture. With the sponsorship of Han and Gupta, the polities they had interacted with the most, Tu'i Tonga joined the world powers two centuries ago, one of the final three polities (along with Seljuk and Inca) to be so declared. Today Tu'i Tonga is enthusiastic about exploration and settlement throughout the solar system, although they prefer to carry out this work in person rather than delegating it to AIs as some other polities do. They have always been known for their diversity of appearances and cultures, which they believe stimulates the broad brainstorming needed to succeed in fundamental research; to preserve this heterogeneity, Tu'i Tonga has come to discourage convergent genetic modifications. Tu'i Tonga is always adopting new technologies to power their spaceships, and they are eager to fund as many sciences as possible. They believe that even the least favored science should have ample support, as one never knows where unexpected breakthroughs may come from in the future. Tu'i Tonga always looks to single out one project to rapidly implement, which they can point to as their signature accomplishment of each sunspot cycle. These projects are typically ambitious and cutting-edge, but they serve as a way to rally public support and enthusiasm for science and exploration.
