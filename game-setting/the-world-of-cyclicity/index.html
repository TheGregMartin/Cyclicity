The world of Cyclicity

Fifteen polities—massive super-nations (see the list), each of them both benevolent and ambitious—cover 95% of the area of the world’s four habitable continents. Billions of people enjoy the benefits of technological advancement and the global stability this confederation of polities provides. Polities continue to seek advantage through espionage, commercial manipulation, leverage over trade markets, and the occasional outright theft of property; but warfare itself is a thing of the past, and any polity or splinter faction that attempts violence is economically crippled by global cooperation.

It was a long journey to this prosperous situation from a prehistory where Homo sapiens emerged as the dominant hominid species on Earth. Much of the credit for this admirable environment goes to Malmani, a prophet and activist who lived seventeen centuries ago. Malmani, and later prophets chosen to resemble them, visited specially selected centers of power, promising to bless these civilizations with secrets of durability and health if the leaders adopted their ideology of peace and equality and the appreciation of science. One by one (see the list), these local powers accepted Malmani’s teachings and prospered, growing into many of today’s polities; the few youngest polities were not visited by a Malmani prophet but rather were mentored by polities who had been.

Over the centuries, Malmani’s philosophy combined with biological scientific advancements has indeed provided longevity: human lifespans can now reach nearly 200 years, and human memory and oral tradition are valued equally with physical archives. Malmanism has also led to the eradication of discrimination and oppression. Indeed, the fact that Malmani was non-binary in gender confers a respected privilege upon all non-binary people today.

All fifteen polities continue to embrace the advancement of science and the support of theoretical research for the betterment of their populations. New generations of materials and construction techniques are complemented by ever more powerful energy sources. Space exploration is active and expanding, with a small colony on the moon, space stations (several orbiting Earth and also one orbiting each of Venus and Mars) with rotating scientific staff, and hundreds of individuals or small teams exploring the solar system for profitable resources. Genetic advances improves both agriculture and the quality of life worldwide, and personal genetic engineering and cybernetic enhancement are beginning to become accessible to whole populations rather than the few with financial access. Artificial intelligences, already trusted to administer complex systems within their trained specialties, are starting to exhibit broader competency and sensibility, stimulating a global discussion about their sentience and rights.

When the dangers of climate change were predicted and successfully avoided a century ago, the prevailing feeling was that humanity was prepared to meet any challenge. However, in the past decades, solar flares have grown more and more massive; in particular, at the end of every sunspot cycle of 9–14 years’ duration, the flares are so strong and disruptive that electronics become dangerously unreliable for days or sometimes frightening weeks at a time. In response, the polities collaborated on the ten-year construction of the Orbital Research Installation (ORI), a shielded next-generation space station designed to be the center of research and decision-making in all areas of science.

The body in charge of the ORI is the Interpolity Science Association (ISA), which represents the entire population of the solar system. Your six polities have been chosen to send ambassadors to form the Directorate of the ISA, situated in person on the space station; together you will be the ultimate decision-making and agenda-setting authority for the most important scientific initiatives. Its highest priority is very explicit: combine all the world’s resources and ingenuity to find a solution to the worsening solar flares … before they cause an unrecoverable collapse of the infrastructure of civilization.
