Tutorial branch points #4–6

This portion of the tutorial uses the branch point cards 02.5, 2B.3, and 2B.4 in some order.

  1. Greg: I’m going to say a few more words, and then you can play through the next three branch point cards however you like! That will give you a chance to practice the rules, ask any questions that come up, and also just have a little fun. (Speaking of rules, don’t forget that you can remove drones from your Drone Factory or Mining Operations any time you want.) First I’ll talk a little about how you might decide on your goals for a given game.
  2. Greg: First, your goals within a game could be determined by your objective cards (two Classified Polity Objectives, and one Supply Contracts for your polity). We’ve seen that some of these objectives can depend on whether branch point decisions take a large number of months or a small number of months; I want to mention that the wording of the branch point can sometimes suggest which of the outcomes will take longer, even if there’s no explicit indication.
  3. Greg: They could also be determined by the Polity Achievements on the right side of your polity screen, which are goals you can try to achieve throughout the campaign; for each one, if you achieve it enough times, you’ll get an installation or some other treat.
  4. Greg: There are some other long-term goals as well on your polity screen, to the left of your polity's description. Your Polity Disposition contains the names of six of the Classified Polity Objective cards, which you can think of as the objectives that are “the most in character” for your polity’s background. Any time you choose one or more of those six, you get to mark a box in its row; completing its row gives you additional Progress Points, which we'll take about when we get to the end of this tutorial game.
  5. Greg: There's also your polity’s Narrative Achievement above those. If you see the story going in a direction that might have something to do with that phrase, you could try to make sure that outcome happens—your polity’s citizens will reward you with praise and some mechanical treat!
  6. Greg: Together, all these goals are the ways you can shape your character throughout this campaign. You could try to hit your polity screen goals as consistently as possible; or you could take objective cards that suit your mood in a particular game; or you can simply roleplay your ISA ambassador and see what happens! Your polity’s past can influence your future choices, but ultimately you make your own decisions.
  7. Greg: Now let’s play! One favor I’ll ask is that you always say out loud the action you’re taking on each of your turns; I’d like to keep track of everything as part of getting feedback about the game.

[Greg shuffles the branch point deck, takes the bottom card from it, and reads it aloud. The players play through the branch point vote, then reclaim their drones the end of voting; the Sponsor reads the outcome aloud, and the months tracker and sliders on the board get updated.]

  1. Greg: Remember to check your Drone Recycling row to see if you gain a drone due to the number of months in the branch point outcome! We should also remember to check installations and rule cards that are triggered by branch points, such as this Tutorial Tracker card.
  2. Greg: This branch point outcome has an installation symbol; this means that the installation in question goes to the Sponsor of that decision. They can put that installation in any Zone they want, as long as the placement obeys the “gravity rules”.
  3. Greg: Just as a rough guideline for choosing which Zones to put installations in: putting one in your Inner Zone uses up a little of your Drone Recycling capacity; but as this Monstrous Solar Flare card hints at, your Outer Zone installations will be a little harder to protect against natural disasters (or opportunistic rivals…). Your Middle Zone will become a compromise between safety and productivity.
  4. [If it ever becomes relevant, Greg mentions that sliders can never go above 16 or below 0.]

[The players play through two more branch point votes. After they are both finished, Greg points out that the Tutorial Tracker card instructs them to open envelope U.]
