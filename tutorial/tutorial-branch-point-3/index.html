Tutorial branch point #3

This portion of the tutorial uses the branch point card 02.4.

  1. Greg: Let’s go on to the third branch point card!
  2. Barney: [reads the third third point card aloud]
  3. Greg: I’ll put the 0 and 1 tokens next to the cyberneticsand energy sourcessliders, as reminders.
  4. Barney: I’ll vote for option 0, because [make something up]. I deploy my Voting tile, and I’ll put two drones on it.
  5. Jason: Let’s see if I can make some friends over here on Cooperative Power; I’ll put a drone on it, and deploy my Abstain tile.
  6. Graham: Good, Jason didn’t grab the Surveillance Network spot, so I get to use it! I put three of my available drones on that card, and another drone from behind my screen on the corner of the Sponsor tile. I don’t deploy a tile just yet.
  7. Vivien: I might as well use the action spot on this cool installation I got—I put three drones there, and I’ll get two extra Victory Points at the end of this game! And I deploy my Abstain tile as well.
  8. Janelle: Well, since I remember there’s an actual vote going on—I’ll vote for option 1 because [make something up]. I deploy my Voting tile, and I’ll put one drone on it.
  9. Katie: I’ll vote with three drones for option 0, because [make something up]. That means I get the Sponsor tile as well.
  10. Greg: Note that Graham’s drone on the corner of the Sponsor tile goes along for the ride; we’ll see that can affect the order of play in interesting ways.
  11. Barney: I’ll play a drone on the Sustainable Deployment card. If the vote comes back around to me I still have the option of adding drones to my Voting tile.
  12. Greg: Note that the Sustainable Deployment card now has a face-up drone on it, so nobody else can use that card during this branch point vote.
  13. Jason: Everyone’s getting all fancy; I’m just placing a drone on my Drone Factory. I’d be happy to put another drone on Cooperative Power, but I already have a drone there.
  14. Greg: It has now rotated to Graham’s turn to take an action—even though he has a delayed turn still waiting for him on the Sponsor tile! Since this is Graham’s “first” turn, he must deploy either a Voting tile or an Abstain tile.
  15. Graham: I’ll vote one drone for option 1, because [make something up]. That will keep my options open for later in this vote.
  16. Vivien and Janelle simultaneously: Let’s do … Cooperative Power! [they each place one drone on that card]
  17. Greg: We’ve now reached the Sponsor tile; so now Graham gets to take the turn he claimed with the Surveillance Network action, starting by taking his drone back from the Sponsor tile.
  18. Graham: Plot twist! I’m adding four more drones to my vote, which puts option 1 ahead six drones to five right now. That makes me the current Sponsor as well.
  19. Greg: If Graham’s action hadn’t moved the Sponsor tile, it would have been Katie’s turn which would have ended the vote since she had the Sponsor tile. But since the tile moved, the vote hasn’t yet reached the Sponsor tile—so it keeps going with Katie’s turn!
  20. Katie: Great, I’ll put two drones on the Patent Office card and claim the Patent Agent tile from Janelle.
  21. Barney: I think I see how this could go … I’m going to add one more drone to my Voting tile.
  22. Jason: You all have fun voting—I’ll just cheerfully add another drone to my Drone Factory.
  23. Greg: We’ve finally reached the Sponsor tile, so the voting round is over. And the two sides are tied, 6 drones to 6—
  24. Katie: —and I get to decide ties, and I know what I want! I want option 0 to win the vote. And, since that makes the final Sponsor of this vote either me or Barney, and we’re tied with 3 drones each, I say that’s me as well!
  25. Barney: Ah well, I had a good run….
  26. Greg: Now let’s reclaim all our drones before processing the outcome of that vote.
  27. Graham: At least I get all of my voting drones back since my side lost the vote, and of course Janelle does too. I also get one of my Surveillance Network drones back—the other two go into my reserve.
  28. Barney: Thanks to having taken the Sustainable Deployment action, I get to reclaimall three of my voting drones! Since that action has the dark drone symbol, I turn my drone upside down on the Sustainable Deployment card, where it will stay the rest of the game.
  29. Greg: In this case, since the Sustainable Deployment card has two copies of its action, someone (even Barney again perhaps) can use that action a second time this game.
  30. Katie: I reclaim one of my voting drones and one of my drones from the Patent Office card; but the other three drones go into my reserve.
  31. Vivien: Two of these drones on my installation go into my reserve; because it’s a dark drone action, the third drone stays upside down on the installation—I can only do that action once per game.
  32. Greg: Finally, Jason, Vivien, and Janelle all take their single drones back from the Cooperative Power card; since there were at least three drones on that card at the end of the vote, each of them also gains a drone from their reserve.
  33. Jason, Vivien, and Janelle simultaneously: Cooperative Power!
  34. Greg: Now let’s process the results of the vote; Katie, flip that branch point card to its 0 outcome.
  35. Katie: [read the 0 outcome text from the back of the card]
  36. Greg: As we read these icons left to right, we’ll get to learn a few more of the rules. First, we definitely move the months tracker 17 months forward. But we also get to see how the Drone Recycling on our installation mats works (not Drone Factory). Note that you have a row of numbers next to your Drone Recycling spot (this is the Inner Zone row), some of which might be covered up by installations. If any of you see the number 17 on an uncovered spot in this row, you gain one drone from your reserve. I believe that’s Katie, Jason, and Graham in this case.
  37. Greg: In general, whenever you see the number of months indicated in a branch point outcome, you’ll always check the visible part of the row beside your Drone Recycling; if you see that number on that row, you gain a drone from your reserve. (Right now the numbers in the other rows are inactive, but you might guess that could change in the future….)
  38. Greg: Another rule we get to learn now concerns these momentum markers. Any time a branch point outcome says to move a slider up, and that slider has a positive momentum marker on it—that slider goes up one additional space as a result of its momentum. In this situation, both genetics and cybernetics have positive momenum; and so the genetics slider goes up 3 spaces rather than 2, and the cybernetics slider goes up 5 spaces rather than 4. (Note that the bonus is only one extra space regardless of whether the positive momentum is +1 or +2.)
  39. Greg: As usual, we set both those sliders’ momentums to +2 since the branch point outcome raised them, and we lower all other sliders’ momentum by 1. Here’s the other rule about momentum: if a slider should have its momentum lowered but its momentum is already at –2, then the slider itself goes down a space instead. So in this case, three sliders actually go down one space—artificial intelligence, energy sources, and theoretical research. (And they stay endangered with their –2 momentums … consistently neglected sciences definitely feel the pain.)
  40. Greg: The final consequence of this vote is to open another envelope, 2B!
