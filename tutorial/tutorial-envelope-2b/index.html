So goals within a game can be determined by your objective cards (Classified Polity Objectives and polity Supply Contracts). Can also be determined by the Polity Achievements on the right side of your polity screen.

Then there are some long-term goals as well to the left of your polity's description. Your Polity Disposition contains the names of six of these objective cards. Any time you choose one or more of those six, you get to mark a box in its row, and completing its row gives you additional Progress Points, which we'll take about when we get to the end of this tutorial game.

There's also the narrative achievement: if you see the story going in the direction of that phrase, you can try to make sure that outcome happens—your polity’s citizens will reward you with praise and some mechanical treat!

Together, all these goals are the ways you shape your character throughout this campaign. You could try to hit your polity screen goals as consistently as possible; or you could take objective cards that suit your mood in a particular game; or you can simply roleplay your ISA ambassador and see what happens!
