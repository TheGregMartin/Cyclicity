Tutorial branch point cards

  • pre 4: Introduce objective goals here, have them take supply ship complement objectives
      • Pragmatist, Theoretician (6; clash)
      • Extremist, Spendthrift (5; clash)
      • Geneticist, Restrictor (4)
      • Balancer, Idler (4)
      • Patron, Specialist (3)
      • Concentrator, Enabler (2)
  • Cards 4–6: let them actually vote their opinions
    • Have all six sliders be possible outcomes on these three cards, and suggest that people play their polities
    • Point out that sometimes the wording suggests whether the decision will take more months or fewer months
    • Mention that sliders can’t go above 16 or below 0
    • Have a rule card with three tick boxes to trigger card 7, as well as a supply ship tax on zone-III installations to teach idea that they’re a little less safe out there
  • Seventh card: decision on redecorating (old vs. new polities)
    • This will teach the 6 + 6 sunspot cards mechanic
  • Last eighth card to trigger endgame
  • Run through endgame scoring
