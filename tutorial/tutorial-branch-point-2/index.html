Tutorial branch point #2

This portion of the tutorial uses the branch point card T.3.

  1. Greg: Let’s go on to the second branch point card!
  2. Barney: [reads the second branch point card aloud]
  3. Greg: I’ll put the 0 and 1 tokens next to the materials scienceand theoretical researchsliders, as reminders. Whoever is the Sponsor from the previous vote goes first in a new vote.
  4. Barney: I’ll vote for option 0, because [make something up]. I deploy my Voting tile, and I’ll put one drone on it.
  5. Jason: I want to try this Surveillance Network action, it sounds sneaky!
  6. Greg: It is! Surveillance Network is a way of delaying your turn until later, so that you have more information at your disposal when you take it. This is the one exception to the rule that you must deploy a Voting or Abstain tile on your first turn—because really you’re postponing your first turn.
  7. Jason: It looks like I need to place three drones from behind my screen on the card to use that action.
  8. Greg: That’s right. And a general rule of Cyclicity is, nobody can add any drones to a card when there are any face-up drones already on it. So this is the only Surveillance Network action that will happen during this branch point vote.
  9. Greg: The result of the Surveillance Network action is: instead of deploying a tile, Jason places a drone from behind his screen on this cornerof the Sponsor tile, to indicate that his turn has been delayed.
  10. Jason: So you need four drones behind your screen to take this action in the first place.
  11. Graham: I’m not scared to give my opinion now! I’ll deploy my 1 Voting tile and place one drone on it, because [make something up].
  12. Vivien: You read my mind, Graham—I’m also voting one drone for option 1, because [make something up].
  13. Janelle: I’ll try this other non-voting action, the Patent Office. So I deploy my Abstain tile, and I place two of my available drones on the card.
  14. Greg: Congratulations Janelle, you are the first Patent Agent! Having this tile means that you will decide any ties that arise during branch point votes or their outcomes.
  15. Katie: I’m with Barney on this one—I’ll vote one drone for option 0, because [make something up].
  16. Greg: Note that the Sponsor tile never moved because nobody ever put more drones on their Voting tile than Barney did initially. Normally that would mean the vote ends now, since we reached the Sponsor tile; but Jason has a drone on that tile which means it’s now his turn! Take that drone back behind your screen and take whatever action you want.
  17. Katie: Hold on Jason: I’ll bribe you one supply ship to vote for option 0.
  18. Vivien: I can do better—I’ll bribe you two supply ships to vote for option 1.
  19. Janelle: Jason, I’ll bribe you three supply ships not to vote at all!
  20. Jason: Sold! I’ll take those supply ships and deploy my Abstain tile.
  21. Greg: In Cyclicity, any bargains made with supply ships are binding. Don’t worry, you can bluff and break promises the rest of the time.
  22. Jason: I still have to choose a non-voting action … I guess the only choices left are putting a drone on my Drone Factory or Mining Operations. I’ll choose Drone Factory since I just got a bunch of supply ships from my good friend Janelle.
  23. Greg: We’ve now reached the Sponsor tile, so the voting is over … and there’s a tie, two drones to two! Which means—
  24. Janelle: —which means I get to decide the winner, so it’s time for me to accept the bribes!
  25. Barney: Okay then—Katie and I will give you one supply ship each to decide in favor of option 0.
  26. Vivien: I’m still willing to pay for option 1! I’ll give you three supply ships, Janelle.
  27. Katie: How about Barney and I give you two supply ships each and we’ll call it a day?
  28. Janelle: That works for me, it means I got a little profit out of this whole sequence! Hand me those supply ships; I declare option 0 to be the winner of this vote.
  29. Greg: Okay! Note that in this case Barney, the person with the Sponsor tile was on the winning side of the vote; so he is this decision’s final Sponsor (it doesn’t matter that he was tied with Katie for most drones on their Voting tiles).
  30. Graham: Since Vivien and I were on the losing side of the vote, we get all of our drones back from our Voting tiles no matter what.
  31. Katie: And in this case, Barney and I only used one drone each—so we get those drones back and don’t have to return any drones to our reserves, even though we won the vote.
  32. Greg: Note that the drone-retrieval rules apply to other actions too!
  33. Jason: Right, I put three drones on Surveillance Network; so I take one back behind my screen but I have to put the other two back in my reserve.
  34. Janelle: And my Patent Agent action “cost” me one drone but I get the other one back.
  35. Greg: Barney, flip that branch point card by the 0 and let’s see what the results are.
  36. Barney: [read the 0 outcome text from the back of the card]
  37. Greg: First we move the months marker 25 months—that’s two years and one extra month. Note that this outcome increases a second slider, cybernetics, in addition to materials science! This happens sometimes, and there might be a clue in the branch point text when it is going to happen.
  38. Greg: The materials science slider goes up 3 and the cybernetics slider goes up 1, and both of them get a +2 momentum marker as a result. All other sliders have their momentum reduced by 1, so the genetics momentum falls to +1 and the other three momentums fall to –2. (We’ll see the effects of momentum in the next branch point of the tutorial.)
  39. Greg: Most excitingly, we get to open an envelope! In this case we’re opening envelope 02 rather than envelope 01; this is how your decisions lead to persistent changes in the game world and its ongoing stories. This envelope is going to teach us more of Cyclicity’s rules, so there are lots of goodies inside it….
