Second Stage Transition—Conclusion

When all players have finished recording their allocations for the third round, turn all their third-round cards face up for everyone to see.

It’s recommended for players to pair up and quickly check the arithmetic of each other’s drone allocations. If a player has spent more supply ships than they had available, their drone allocations will be carried out as fully as possible starting from the top of the card.

Determining final control

Start by determining final control of the outpost with the most total drones on it. In the event of a tie, the player with the first-place ranking card chooses which outpost to address. Any players who allocated drones to this outpost simultaneously add their drones now (keeping them separate from the drones that were already there, so that it can still be determined how many drones were placed in the first pair of rounds). The two players with the most drones on the outpost at this point are now co-sponsors of that outpost.

  • Any ties are broken in favor of the player(s) in control of the outpost at the end of the second round, or (if needed) in favor of the player(s) who had more drones on the outpost at the end of the second round. If a tie persists, the player(s) in control of the outpost at the end of the second round decide(s) which of the tied players share(s) control of it.

When the two polities co-sponsoring this outpost have been determined, remove all of their drones from the other two outposts (and remove all other players’ drones from the first outpost). Then determine final control of the remaining outpost that has the most total drones on it, in the same way as the first outpost. The third outpost is automatically co-sponsored by the two polities who are not co-sponsors of either of the first two outposts.

Give each polity their Polity Outpost tiles. Next, determine the nature of each outpost’s co-sponsorship and put the correspondoing Central Outpost tile into play:

  • If neither co-sponsoring polity placed a drone on the Seize Authority card, give the pair of polities the Cooperative tile corresponding to their outpost.
  • If exactly one co-sponsoring polity placed a drone on the Seize Authority card, give the pair of polities the Authority Seized tile corresponding to their outpost.
  • If both co-sponsoring polities placed a drone on the Seize Authority card, give the pair of polities the Malfunctioning tile corresponding to their outpost.

All these Central Outpost tiles have rules on the back that describe how the Polity Outpost tiles are positioned and what their action spots do. In every game from now on, the two polities co-sponsoring each outpost must be seated next to each other.

Finally, determine control of all installations. Starting with the player with the 1st-place ranking card:

  • Any other players who allocated drones to installations on the 1st-place player’s mat add their drones to those installations now.
  • The 1st-place player then allocates drones to their own installations (only the ones with foreign drones on them are relevant). Final control is dictated by whoever has the most drones, with ties broken the same was as for stations.
  • Any installations on the 1st-place player’s mat that are controlled by other players are given to those players, keeping them separate from their installation mats for now.

The same procedure is then resolved for the 2nd-place player, and so on.

Restoring installation mats

Remove all installations from your installation mat, keeping track of which ones were on arcs 1, 2, and 3, and which ones came from outposts or other players. Flip your installation mat back to its main side. Place your installations on your mat as follows:

  • All installations from your 1 arc and 2 arc go into your Inner Zone. If there isn’t enough room in your Inner Zone, choose which extra installation(s) to put in your Middle Zone.
  • All installations from your 3 arc go into your Middle Zone. If there isn’t enough room in your Middle Zone, choose which extra installation(s) to put in your Outer Zone.
  • Any installations you stole from other players go into your Outer Zone. If there isn’t enough room in your Outer Zone, choose which extra installation(s) to remove from the game.

Remember that installations must always go as far to the left as possible in their zones, in any order you choose, and that the Gravity Rule is always in effect.

Congratulations—civilization has now entered its Interstellar Stage. Discard all Second Stage Transition cards, and either save the game for the next session or start the next game immediately!
