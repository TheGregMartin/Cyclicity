Distribute rankings and Progress Points

All players are ranked in 1st through 6th place based on their total number of Victory Points, with higher totals given better rankings. There are no ties in these rankings; the End-Game Information card describes how ties are broken between players with the same number of Victory Points.

Once these rankings have been determined, deal out the 1st–6th place ranking cards to the appropriate players; each player should keep their ranking card until the start of the next game.

If this is not the first game of the campaign, there will be a Classified Polity Objective card being used as a rule card in the bottom right slot on the board; return this card to the Classified Polity Objectives deck.

The 1st-place player must choose which one of their two Classified Polity Objective cards will be used as a rule in the next game (the rules are found in the upside-down text at the bottom of the objective cards). Place this card face up and upside down in the bottom right slot on the board, and cover it with the Objective Rule cover tile (with its action spots face up).

All players immediately mark the number of Progress Points indicated on their ranking card.

  • Start by marking the few boxes in the top row
  • Then mark the boxes in the grid, starting from the top row, from left to right in each row. In the grid, make sure to mark the boxes in a way that they can be marked again a second time.
  • Anytime you mark a box with a number, acquire the corresponding Polity Reward.
