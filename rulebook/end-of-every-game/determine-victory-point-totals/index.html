Determine Victory Point totals

Remove the months marker from the board; the months tracker now becomes the scoring track. All players’ scoring markers start on the 0 space. Each player updates their scoring marker every time they gain Victory Points (VP).

  • To avoid crowding near the 0 space, it might be convenient for each player to keep their standee off the board until the first time they score Victory Points.
  • Other than the very last scoring step, players can record their VP simultaneously.
First, go through all end-of-game triggers on rule cards and installations, including marking the counter cards. (If the order should ever matter, use the same order as in the start-of-every-game rules.)

  • Some of these effects might trigger off of conditions that are not yet known, such as player rankings for the most drones or supply ships. Make a note of any such unresolved effects and keep track of them through the scoring process. The same applies to the next two items.

Next, each player should check the Polity Disposition and Polity Achievements sections of their polity screens.

  • In your Polity Disposition section, mark the leftmost empty box (if any) under each row corresponding to a Classified Polity Objective you are holding. If you mark the rightmost box, immediately (single-)mark the indicated number of Progress Points.
    • If you would mark a box in a row that is already completely marked, thus “overfilling” that row: Instead gain Victory Pointsequal to the number of Progress Pointsthat row awarded you (but do not gain further Progress Points).
  • In your Polity Achievements section, mark the leftmost empty box (if any) of each achievement whose condition was fulfilled this game. If you mark the rightmost box, immediately claim the indicated polity reward or installation.
    • If you would mark a box in a row that is already completely marked, thus “overfilling” that row: Instead gain 1 Victory Point.

Next, all players count how many drones were behind their screen at the end of the game. The players with the most, 2nd-most, and 3rd-most drones gain 4 VP, 2 VP, and 1 VP, respectively (as indicated on the End-Game Information card).

  • Drones on cards, installations, and installation mats do not count for this ranking. However, a player may choose to remove drones from their Drone Factory and/or their Mining Operations any time they wish, including right before calculating these rankings (and they still receive any bonuses those spots supply).
  • All ties are friendly: for example, if two players tied for having the most drones, they would both get 4 VP, and the player with the next-highest drone count would get the third-place reward of 1VP.

Similarly, all players count how many supply ships were behind their screen at the end of the game. Determine which players come in 1st place, 2nd place, and so on for this count. Then, every player earns the number of VP indicated for their ranking on their Supply Contracts card (which depends on its Priority level).

  • Again all ties are friendly: for example, if one player had 9 supply ships, three players had 6 supply ships, and two players had 4 supply ships, there would be one 1st-place finisher, three 2nd-place finishers, and two 5th-place finishers.

Finally, the last sources of Victory Points are the players’ Classified Polity Objective cards. For maximum drama, players count these last VP one player at a time, with the order determined by everyone’s partial VP scores to this point: the player provisionally ranked in 6th place goes first, then the player provisionally ranked in 5th place, and so on.
