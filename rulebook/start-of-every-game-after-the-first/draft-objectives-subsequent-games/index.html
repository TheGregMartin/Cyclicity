Draft objectives (subsequent games)

Shuffle together all Classified Polity Objective cards, except the one being used as the Objective Rule card for this game. Deal them all out face down, starting with the Sponsor and going clockwise (players might receive different numbers of cards).

Drafting Classified Polity Objectives occurs simultaneously over three rounds. In each drafting round, each player may either choose a Classified Polity Objective from the ones they currently hold (keeping it secret), or decline to do so. Each player must end the three rounds with exactly 2 Classified Polity Objectives, so each player will decline to choose a card in exactly one of the three rounds.

  • Important rule: The second chosen Classified Polity Objective cannot have an icon in common (other than the Priority level suitcases) with the first chosen one.

In the first and second round, after all players have chosen a card or declined to do so, all players pass their unchosen Classified Polity Objectives to the player on their left. Players will therefore draft their objectives from different sets of cards in each round.

  • In the third round, In the rare event that all Classified Polity Objectives available to a player share an icon with the objective they already chose, they will have a fourth drafting round using the unchosen cards from the player on their right.

All unchosen Classified Polity Objective cards are returned to the box—they will not be used this game.

Finally, each player chooses their Polity Supply Contracts card whose Priority level brings their total Priority level up to 6.

  • If a player chose two Priority-1 level Classified Polity Objectives, they select their Priority-3 Polity Supply Contracts; this is legal but could put them at a scoring disadvantage.
