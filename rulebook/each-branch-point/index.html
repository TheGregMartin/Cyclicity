Each branch point

  • Draw bottom card from branch point deck. If it’s a special card, do it. Otherwise:
    • Process branch point
    • Remove drones
    • Advance narrative
    • Adjust sliders, momentum, and months counter
    • Put branch point card in discard pile

Process branch point

  • Read front of branch point card aloud (do not look at the back)
  • Place 0 and 1 tokens near the appropriate sliders, to indicate which sliders will go up if that decision is made (sometimes other sliders go up too)
  • Starting from Sponsor and going clockwise, each player either votes or takes one action (updating Sponsor)
    • People deploy their Vote 0, Vote 1, or Abstain tile in the first round; can’t change their mind thereafter, but Vote 0/1 can add to their vote or do a non-vote action each time
    • Legal to take no action, but usually better to at least add a drone to Shipyards
  • Keep going until the Sponsor is reached again
  • Determine winner of vote (Patent Agent breaks ties)

Remove drones

  • Those who voted for winner: take one drone back from voting spot to behind their screen, and discard the rest into their reserves
  • Those who voted for loser: take all drones back from voting spot to behind their screen
  • Repeatable action spots: take one drone back to behind their screen, discard the rest into reserves
  • One-time action spots: instead of taking that one drone back, flip it upside down and leave it there

Advance narrative

  • Flip over branch point card and read appropriate outcome
    • Keep an eye out for Narrative Achievements!
  • Open any envelopes
    • Sponsor places installation in one of their zones (legally)

Adjust sliders and months counter

  • Move sliders (never past 0 or 16)
    • If a slider with positive momentum is moved up, move it up an extra 1
    • Any slider that’s moved up: set its momentum to MAX (+2)
    • Any slider that wasn’t moved up: reduce its momentum by 1, or if it’s already at MIN (–2), move the slider down 1
  • Advance months counter
    • Turn over Intensifying/Critical Sunspots cards; might trigger end of game
