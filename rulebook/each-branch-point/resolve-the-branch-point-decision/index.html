Resolve the branch point decision

The Sponsor or the game facilitator reads aloud the text on the front of the branch point card, then places it on the table for people to refer to. (Nobody should look at the back of the card until voting is finished.)

Place the 0 and 1 tokens near the sliders corresponding to the icons on the branch point card, for visual reminders of which sliders will be increased by the decisions.

Starting from the Sponsor and continuing clockwise, each player takes either a voting action or a non-voting action.

  • They must deploy either a Voting tileor an Abstain tileon their first turn (by moving it from behind their screen to a publicly viewable spot).
  • Exception: a player who takes the Surveillance Networkaction on their first turn is deemed not to have taken a turn yet, and so they wait until their subsequent turn to deploy a tile.

A voting action consists of a player placing one or more drones from behind their screen on their Voting tile (with either the 0 or 1 side up, whichever they want).

  • If it is the player’s first turn of the current branch point, they must deploy their Voting tile (chosen side up) as part of the voting action.
  • If it is the player’s second or later turn of the current branch vote, they cannot take a voting action if they didn’t deploy a Voting tile in their first action; and they cannot change which side of the Voting tile faces up during a branch point.
  • In either case, if the voting action results in the voting player having more drones on their Voting tile than any other player, then the Sponsor tile is moved to the right of the voting player’s polity screen.

Non-voting actions are all other actions, including using action spots on cards, installations, or installation mats.

  • If it is the player’s first turn of the current branch point, they must deploy their Abstain tile as part of the non-voting action.
  • Action spots on cards are available to all players, but action spots on a player’s installations or installation mat are available only to that player themselves.
  • Passing is a valid non-voting action (although it is almost always better for a player to place a drone on their Drone Factory or Mining Operations action spot).

Players continue to take turns in clockwise order until the vote reaches the Sponsor tile, that is, until the next player to act would be the player holding the Sponsor tile. At that point, the player holding the Sponsor tile does not take a turn, and the branch point vote is finished.

Count all drones on Voting tiles. If there are more total drones on one option (0 or 1) than the other, then the option with more drones has won the branch point vote.

  • If the two options have the same number of total drones, then the player holding the Patent Agent tiledecides which option wins the branch point vote.
  • If the player with the Sponsor tile voted for the winning option, then they are the Sponsor of the branch point decision.
  • If the player with the Sponsor tile voted for the losing option, the Sponsor tile is given to the player who voted for the winning option with the most drones, and they become the Sponsor of the branch point decision. If multiple players are tied for the most votes on the winning side, the Patent Agent decides who becomes the Sponsor.
  • If nobody deployed a Voting tile at all, the Patent Agent decides which option wins the branch point vote and becomes the Sponsor themselves.
