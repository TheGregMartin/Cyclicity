Remove drones

After concluding a branch point vote, all players remove the drones they played as follows:

First, every player who voted for the losing side of the vote retrieves all of the drones on their Voting tile, putting them back behind their screen to be used again later.

In every other spot where players put drones, including the Voting tiles of the winning side, the standard rule of Cyclicity is: The player takes one drone back behind their screen, and returns any drones in excess of one to their drone reserve.

Exceptions to this standard rule:

  • If the action spot has a dark droneicon instead of a light droneicon: The single drone that would normally go back behind the player’s screen is instead flipped upside down and left on the action spot.
  • If the action spot has a stackicon: All drones played on the action spot remain there.
