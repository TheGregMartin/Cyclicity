Rulebook

Campaign setup

  • Every player picks a polity, and an ORI ambassador (whatever name they want)
    • information includes founding date (pre- or post-Malmani); oldest = starting Sponsor; second oldest = starting Patent Agent
    • polity screen includes priority 0/1/2/3 supply contracts cards
    • 24 drones (marked as belonging to that polity)
  • Place main slider/rule board in centre of table, with sliders and momentum markers
  • Open packs of supply ships (1s and 5s)
  • Open envelope S, which includes cards that tell you what to do:
    • Place 4 trigger cards for genetics, energy sources, theoretical research, and cybernetics, as well as their explainer card
    • Also place Patent Office and Network Surveillance cards, and +3 drones fake objective with its cover tile
    • Story card 10 in storyline 6
    • Cards 11, 12, 13 to shuffle and form the branch point deck

Start of the first game/solar cycle

Board setup

  • Place the months marker on the 0 spot on the month tracker
  • Shuffle the 6 Critical Sunspots cards face down, then place them at the top of the month tracker. Do the same with the Intensifying Sunspots cards, putting them on top of the Critical Sunspots cards.
  • Place sliders on the 5/4/3/2/1/0 left to right (Materials Science at 5, Cybernetics at 0). Sliders start with momentum 0 (no momentum markers); put the momentum markers near the board for easy access.
  • Open envelope S and follow its instructions.

Player setup

  • All players place their polity screens in front of them, with their drone reserve hidden behind it.
  • All players place their installation mats in front of their screens, facing themselves but visible to all players.
  • Look for all start-of-solar-cycle triggers and resolve them. For the first game these will be:
    • the “ISA Inauguration” card at the bottom left of the board
    • the spot between the Drone Factory and Mining Operations action locations on their installation mat
    • so each player will begin with 11 drones and 8 supply ships

Draft objectives

  • Choose objectives
    • Shuffle all objectives, deal them all out starting with Sponsor (some might get more than others)
    • Three drafting rounds. In each drafting round, each player may choose 0 or 1 objectives from the ones they currently have. Each player must end the three rounds with a total of 2 objectives.
      • The two chosen objectives can’t have an icon in common (other than the Priority level suitcases).
    • Each player selects one face down in front of them (or not); when all have selected, pass the remaining ones face down to the left.
      • In the rare event that all objectives to choose from share a symbol, add the leftovers from their right-hand neighbour after they’ve selected, etc.
    • All unselected objectives returned to box
    • Each player chooses their supply ship objective that brings their total Priority level up to 6
      • If they chose two Priority-1 level objectives, they select their Priority-3 supply ship objective; this is legal but could put them at a scoring disadvantage

Finishing touches

  • Open envelope 01 and follow its instructions.
  • Sponsor reveals the first branch point card, and we're off!

Start of every game/solar cycle after the first

Board setup

  • Place the months marker on the 0 spot [first uncrossed-out spot] on the marker track
  • Shuffle the 6 Critical Sunspots cards face down, then place them at the top of the month tracker. Do the same with the Intensifying Sunspots cards, putting them on top of the Critical Sunspots cards.
  • Place sliders on 5/4/3/2/1/0, in the order they finished last game. Sliders start with momentum 0 (no momentum markers) unless otherwise specified.
  • Set up all previously revealed cards in their spots on the board.

Player setup

  • All players set up their installations on their installation mat. Remember, each installation must remain in its zone, and all installations must be as far left as possible. (The order of installations within a zone is not important.)
  • Go through all start-of-solar-cycle triggers (including claiming drones and supply ships)
    • if order ever matters: story rules top to bottom, then top slider rules left to right (except whole new game-start envelopes) and then bottom slider rules left to right, then player rules clockwise starting with Sponsor
    • return 1st/2nd/etc. cards
  • If any game-start envelope was placed last game, open it now.

Draft objectives

Same as in the first game.

Finishing touches

  • The player with the Network Outage card places it on a valid rule card, one with the outage icon at the bottom. This is the first bribable action of the game.
  • Sponsor reveals the first branch point card, and we're off!

Every branch point

  • Draw bottom card from branch point deck. If it’s a special card, do it. Otherwise:
    • Process branch point
    • Remove drones
    • Advance narrative
    • Adjust sliders, momentum, and months counter
    • Put branch point card in discard pile

Process branch point

  • Starting from Sponsor and going clockwise, each player either votes or takes one action (updating Sponsor)
    • People deploy their Vote 0, Vote 1, or Abstain tile in the first round; can’t change their mind thereafter, but Vote 0/1 can add to their vote or do a non-vote action each time
    • Legal to take no action, but usually better to at least add a drone to Shipyards
  • Keep going until the Sponsor is reached again
  • Determine winner of vote (Patent Agent breaks ties)

Remove drones

  • Those who voted for winner: take one drone back from voting spot to behind their screen, and discard the rest into their reserves
  • Those who voted for loser: take all drones back from voting spot to behind their screen
  • Repeatable action spots: take one drone back to behind their screen, discard the rest into reserves
  • One-time action spots: instead of taking that one drone back, flip it upside down and leave it there

Advance narrative

  • Flip over branch point card and read appropriate outcome
    • Keep an eye out for Narrative Achievements!
  • Open any envelopes
    • Sponsor places installation in one of their zones (legally)

Adjust sliders and months counter

  • Move sliders (never past 0 or 16)
    • If a slider with positive momentum is moved up, move it up an extra 1
    • Any slider that’s moved up: set its momentum to MAX (+2)
    • Any slider that wasn’t moved up: reduce its momentum by 1, or if it’s already at MIN (–2), move the slider down 1
  • Advance months counter
    • Turn over Intensifying/Critical Sunspots cards; might trigger end of game

Adjust PPGP

Terminology: PPGP stands for Polity Population Genetic Profile, EGO stands for Essential Genome Offset, EGG stands for Essential Genome Gradient (in case I want one term for player-vs.-baseline and another term for player-vs.-player). The four categories of essential genes are Complementary, Duplicate, Fungible, and Polymeric.

  • When taking this action, choose one gene type currently at 25% or more and one gene type currently at 25% or less. Add 1% to the higher gene type by filling in the box above the highest box already filled; and subtract 1% from the lower gene type by filling in the box below the lowest box already filled.
    • Once a gene type’s percentage is increased above 25%, it can never be reduced; and once a gene type’s percentage is reduced below 25%, it can never be increased. That’s not a problem though—there will never be a reason or incentive to both increase and reduce the same gene type. (Trust your polity’s central genetic planners!)
  • In addition to the single drone (which must remain on the PPGP mat until the end of the game), this action also requires double-marking a certain number of single-marked boxes in the grid (always starting from the top row of the grid, from left to right).
    • The number of single-marked boxes that must be double-marked is the new percentage of the higher gene type minus the new percentage of the lower gene type. For example, if the higher gene type is increasing from 27% to 28% and the lower gene type is decreasing from 24% to 23%, the number of boxes that must be double-marked is 28 – 23 = 5.
    • If there are not enough single-marked boxes in the grid, this action cannot be taken.
  • Adjusting PPGP is the only thing that double-marking boxes will be used for, so there’s no need to ration them or hold back.

End of every game/solar cycle

  • Go through all end-of-solar-cycle triggers (in the same order as start-of-cycle), including marking the counter cards
  • Determine winner and rankings
  • Mark polity screens
  • Resolve the counter card rule (empty spots or fully marked cards)
  • Prepare for next game

Determine winner and ranks

  • End-of-game scoring
    • Find who's 1st/2nd/3rd for remaining drone swarms; 4/2/1 VPs
    • Find who's 1st/2nd/3rd for supply ships; calculate VPs based on supply contracts (affected by individual priorities)
    • From last to first in VP order: resolve both their classified objectives
    • Scores are final, determine rankings (ties broken by Patent Agent?)
  • Deal out 1st/2nd/etc. cards to players, to save for start of next game
  • 1st player: choose one of their two classified objective cards and place it as the new winner's objective rule

Mark polity screens

  • Progress Points:
    • Immediately mark the number of Progress Points noted on the rankings card (and any other sources)
      • Start by marking the few boxes in the top row
      • Then mark the boxes in the grid, starting from the top row, from left to right in each row. In the grid, make sure to mark the boxes in a way that they can be marked again a second time.
      • Anytime you mark a box with a number, acquire the corresponding polity reward
  • Polity Disposition: Mark the leftmost empty box (if any) under each row corresponding to a Classified Polity Objective you are holding. If you mark the rightmost box, immediately mark the indicated number of Progress Points.
  • Polity Achievements: Mark the leftmost empty box (if any) of each achievement whose condition was fulfilled this game. If you mark the rightmost box, immediately claim the indicated polity reward or installation.

Prepare for next game

  • Remove branch point discard pile contents from the game. Shuffle any extant event cards back into the branch point deck
  • Put slider markers in order they finished (ties broken by momentum, then left to right)
  • 1st player's other classified objective card, and everyone else's two classified objective cards, go back into the classified objective deck
