Player setup (first game)

Each player places their polity screen in front of them, with their drone reserve hidden behind it. They then place a Voting tile and an Abstain tile behind their polity screen.

Each player places their installation mat in front of their screen, facing themselves but visible to all players.

Look for all start-of-solar-cycle triggers and resolve them. For the first game there will be two of these:

  • the “ISA Inauguration” card at the bottom left of the board
  • the spot between the Drone Factory and Mining Operations action locations on their installation mat

Thus each player will begin with 11 drones (taken from their drone reserve but kept behind their screen) and 8 supply ships (taken from the general supply ship reserve and placed behind their screen).
