Board setup (first game)

Keep the following cards from the tutorial:

  • the 6 Critical Sunspots cards and the 6 Intensifying Sunspots cards
  • the 12 Classified Polity Objective cards
  • the 4 Supply Contracts cards for each player’s polity
  • Patent Office, Surveillance Network, and End-Game Scoring Order

Remove all other tutorial cards from the game.

Open envelope R.

  • Set aside the 6 ranking cardsand the Network Outage cardfor the end of the first game.
  • Combine the 19 Classified Polity Objective cards from envelope R with the 12 Classified Polity Objective cards from the tutorial, to form the Classified Polity Objective deck.

Place the months marker on the 0 spot on the month tracker

Shuffle the 6 Critical Sunspots cards face down, then place them at the top of the month tracker. Do the same with the Intensifying Sunspots cards, putting them on top of the Critical Sunspots cards.

Set the sliders to 6/5/4/3/2/1 left to right (Materials Science at 6, Cybernetics at 1). Sliders start with momentum 0 (no momentum markers); place the momentum markers near the board for easy access. Also place the 0 and 1 tokens near the sliders.

Open envelope S and follow its instructions. It will have you put 7 new cards, as well as Patent Office, Surveillance Network, and End-Game Scoring Order, in spots at the bottom of the board corresponding to the icons printed on the cards

  • top row, left to right: Awaiting Discoveries, Patent Office, blank, Genetics Counter, Energy Sources Counter, Theoretical Research Counter, Cybernetics Counter
  • bottom row, left to right: ISA Inauguration, Surveillance Network, three blank spaces, End-Game Scoring Order, and Counter Processing
