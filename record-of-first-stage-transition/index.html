Record of First Stage Transition

Here are the rules of the First Stage Transition, for posterity.

First Stage Transition

This event is the transition into the Second Stage of the campaign. The event will take place over three rounds, where each player maneuvers for control of the four major stations, secures their existing installations … and possibly steals other players’ installations during the scramble. Most of the mechanics of this transition are of the “have the most drones there first” variety, like how control of the Sponsor token works.

Setup

Before beginning the event, mark one box on every installation with the stage transition icon.

Remove all installations from your mat, keeping track of which ones are in your Inner, Middle, and Outer Zones. Flip the installation mat upside down, oriented so that the numbers are readable to you (with 1 closest to you). Put your installations on this new side of the mat, with Inner Zone installations on the 1 arc, Middle Zone installations on the 2 arc, and Outer Zone installations on the 3 arc. (Their horizontal placement doesn’t matter; it might help to have the installations spread as far apart as possible.)

All players position their installation mats around the cards and supply ship reserve on the table, so that the six mats form a hexagon around them. Place any installation explanation cards adjacent to your installation mat, available for everyone to see, as well as your ranking card from the previous game. Keep your drone reserve behind your polity screen.

Description of play

At the beginning of each of the three rounds, each player starts with a certain amount of supply ships that they can allocate during that round. That amount (which is the same for each of the rounds) is 8, plus half the number of installations they start with (rounded down), plus half their polity’s EGO. Let’s record that number of supply ships for each player now.

On a player’s turn, they may spend any or all of their supply ships for that round to place drones on various stations and/or installations. The number of supply ships needed to place a drone on various locations is listed on the Cost Reference card in the middle of the table, which will be read aloud now.

Once a station has at least one drone on it, it is provisionally controlled by the player who has placed the most drones there so far. Put that player’s drones on top of the station and other players’ drones beside that station, to help track who controls it at the moment. Control only passes to a different player if that player has more drones on the station; tying for the most drones is not enough—the player already controlling it retains control in this case.

Each installation is also controlled by the player who has placed the most drones on it, just like stations—except that an installation with no drones on it is controlled by its owner.

Take a few minutes to look at all the players’ installations, and take note any installations you might be interested in stealing! (It might help to record their actual installation numbers privately.)

The order of play is as follows:

  • In the first round, the player with the 1st-place ranking card goes first, followed by the 2nd-place player, and so on, ending with the 6th-place player.
  • The second round goes in the reverse order: the player with the 6th-place ranking card goes first, followed by the 5th-place player, and so on, ending with the 1st-place player.
  • After the second round has been completed, turn over the card on top of the remainder of Envelope B1, and follow its instructions to carry out the third and final round of this event.

Note: Supply ships cannot change hands during this event—they are only there to determine how many actions a player can take each round. There is no bribing during stage transitions, and any agreements among players are non-binding.

Note: Players are not limited by the number of physical drones in their reserve. They can, for instance, use supply ships themselves in place of drones on their own arc 1 installations.

First Stage Transition—Supply ship counts

Here are the supply ship totals that each player will be able to use in each round:

  • Janelle (Scythia): 12
  • Katie and Russell (Gupta): 12
  • Barney (Oyo): 11
  • Vivien (Inuit): 13
  • Jason (Tu’i Tonga): 12
  • Graham (Achaemenia): 12

First Stage Transition—Conclusion

When all players have finished recording their allocations for the third round, turn all their third-round cards face up for everyone to see.

It’s recommended for players to pair up and quickly check the arithmetic of each other’s drone allocations. If a player has spent more supply ships than they had available, or if they incorrectly recorded any costs, their drone allocations are carried out as fully as possible (with corrected supply ship costs) starting from the top of the card.

Determining final control

Start by determining final control of the ORI station. Any players who allocated drones to the ORI simultaneously add their drones now (keeping them separate from the drones that were already there, so that it can still be determined how many drones were placed in the first pair of rounds). Whoever has the most drones on the ORI at this point is now based in the ORI and in charge of that station. That player receives installation C6, keeping it separate from their installation mat for now.

  • If there is a tie for most drones, the tie is broken in favor of the player who was in control of the ORI at the end of the second round, or (if needed) in favor of the player who had more drones on the ORI at the end of the second round. If a tie persists, the player who was in control of the ORI at the end of the second round decides which of the tied players becomes in charge of it.

Similarly, determine final control of Station α, Station β, and Lunar Base in the same way.

  • The player in charge of Station α receives installation C1. They choose one of the six sliders (representing the scientific area they want to promote) and draw its icon in the dashed box on the installation.
  • The player in charge of Station β receives installation C2 and likewise draws their chosen slider icon on it.
  • The player in charge of Lunar Base receives installations C0 and CF, both of which have corresponding installation rule cards.

Next, determine control of all installations. Starting with the player with the 1st-place ranking card:

  • Any other players who allocated drones to installations on the 1st-place player’s mat add their drones to those installations now.
  • The 1st-place player then allocates drones to their own installations (only the ones with foreign drones on them are relevant). Final control is dictated by whoever has the most drones, with ties broken the same way as for stations.
  • Any installations on the 1st-place player’s mat that are controlled by other players are given to those players, keeping them separate from their installation mats for now.

The same procedure is then resolved for the 2nd-place player, and so on.

Restoring installation mats

Remove all installations from your installation mat, keeping track of which ones were on arcs 1, 2, and 3, and which ones came from controlling stations or other players’ installations. Flip your installation mat back to its main side. Put an Extended Drone Recycling sticker next to the bottom two rows of squares (so that its upper half covers the original Drone Recycling). For the rest of the game, your Inner Zone consists of the bottom two rows of squares on your installation mat. The Middle and Outer Zones remain the same.

Place your installations on your mat as follows:

  • Any installations you received from controlling stations go into your Inner Zone.
  • All installations from your 1 arc and 2 arc go into your Inner Zone. If there isn’t enough room in your Inner Zone, choose which extra installation(s) to put in your Middle Zone.
  • All installations from your 3 arc go into your Middle Zone. If there isn’t enough room in your Middle Zone, choose which extra installation(s) to put in your Outer Zone.
  • Any installations you stole from other players go into your Outer Zone. If there isn’t enough room in your Outer Zone, choose which extra installation(s) to remove from the game.

Remember that installations must always go as far to the left as possible in their zones, in any order you choose. In addition, there must now be at least as many installations in the bottom row of your Inner Zone as there are in the top row of your Inner Zone at all times (an extension of the “gravity rules”).

Congratulations—civilization has now entered its Second Stage. Discard all First Stage Transition cards, and either save the game for the next session or start the next game immediately!
