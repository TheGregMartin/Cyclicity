0: A New Dawn Above Earth. At the beginning of a sunspot cycle, the Interpolity Science Association convenes in orbit for the first time. Its headquarters is a well-appointed suite of rooms in the Orbital Research Installation, a glimmering ring suspended high above the quiet curve of Earth. Envoys from six polities have been chosen to lead civilization through an age of discovery—and of growing peril. The Sun, once a symbol of renewal, has begun to falter in strange and unpredictable rhythms, casting flares that disrupt communication, damage infrastructure, and darken continents. Yet optimism runs high; humanity has never been so united or so capable. As this 1st Cycle begins, hope rises with it that understanding the rhythms of the star will illuminate the path ahead. Here aboard the ORI, science and diplomacy intertwine, and every decision the ISA makes shapes the survival of civilization.
